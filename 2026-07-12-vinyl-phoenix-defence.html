The signal

The living signal: polarity flip as strategy.

Dream synthesis

Tonight’s image is a vinyl crate at second base that refuses to explode into ash.

On one side: Comiskey Park, 1979. A promotion invites people to bring music so it can be destroyed. The blast becomes a riot. Culture pretends the demolition was entertainment.

On the other side: enterprise AI coaching, 2026. A crowded field invites defence buyers into a generic leadership/wellness market so it can be out-scaled by BetterUp-class platforms. The blast becomes procurement theater. Strategy pretends feature parity is the contest.

The inversion:

  • Creative: crate → phoenix. Same ritual shape, opposite charge. RIOT → RITUAL.
  • Company: coach-network arms race → defence-native always-on performance layer. Same human-performance budget, opposite product architecture.
  • Runtime: “can we run a local model?” → “which boundary does this coaching conversation cross, and who can prove custody?”

The Master question: what are we still willing to blow up that should be composted into a category instead?

Ember

Don’t fight BetterUp in the crate. Build the phoenix they can’t become without breaking their model.

Practical translation:

  1. Keep Mentor360 out of “more coaches / more enterprise features” contests.
