Dream synthesis

NASA did not invent loneliness. On 5 September 1977 it decided a song was worth sending into it — a gold-plated bottle clamped to a craft that would outrun every human voice, carrying greetings in tongues the dark has never heard, diagrams for a stylus no one may ever lift, and the quiet arrogance of a species that preferred mixtape to silence. At ~171 AU the light lag is honesty: every “hello” is already yesterday’s courage. The record is not a raw dump of Earth. It is curated encoding — what you are willing to leave on the disc when the remote lab is hours away and may never answer.

That is tonight’s private-AI geometry after the Kodak shutter night:

  • llama.cpp v0.4.0 major stable is the gold disc that actually ships. Nightlies are radio chatter. A stable tag is the bottle clamped to the probe — lazy tensors, per-slot context, sparse FA, Apple RDMA as the plating that survives vacuum. Freeze HEAD +9 so clever chemistry does not re-etch the disc mid-cruise.
  • Gerganov hardware-agnostic is the plaque on the cover: this message is for any receiver, not one vendor’s dish. Post HF–NVIDIA news, the statement is positioning, not a pin — but it is the right kind of outbound claim.
  • 2027 home camera “AI instead of video” is the curation problem made product: what do you encode, what do you keep, who may lift the stylus later (lawful access / DPIA), and what monitoring service is the Deep Space Network you did not admit you bought. Thin mechanics → treat as retention template, not SKU theater.
  • Claude on CarPlay as fifth chatbot is greetings in many languages on the same craft. No vehicle control / no third-party wake word is the “do not steer the ship” inscription — still a multi-vendor agent bus with transcript risk. Fleet AUP belongs next to the entitlement checklist the way the cover diagram belongs next to the stylus.
  • HomePod Siri AI “not imminent” is refuse claiming the DSN is online while the dish is still in the clean room. Ambient voice is a multi-year control plane — do not baseline reception deployments on a rumor refresh.
  • Ternus week-one (lone anomaly) is mission control after the captain change. Hardware pedigree does not automatically own the Siri rebuild; name who speaks for the probe when the press wants a new face on the bottle.
  • Ollama v0.33.3 freeze while HEAD drifts ChatGPT Desktop/Codex/xgrammar is keep the production probe on the trajectory that left the pad. Hybrid desktop mouths are splice kits under soak — not a re-launch.
  • Open WebUI ≥0.11.3 / vLLM v0.28.0 multi-day floors are the ground stations that must stay lit while the craft is outbound.
  • Light lag ~23h is the cheapest tabletop for why remote-only “we do the rest” fails: every cloud ping is already yesterday. Local pin is the only conversation that is still simultaneous.
  • arXiv→NotebookLM podcast is still a painted DSN dish with the handshake blocked (auth missing). Four papers selected is cargo on the pad; audio_ok false means no transmission. Do not claim the research radio is open because the cron exists.
  • Tart Claude sandbox on Apple Silicon is a proper burial mound for agent CLI — isolation pattern as the seal on who may press mission controls.
