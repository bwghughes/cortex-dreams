Dream synthesis

Sweden did not invent roads. On Dagen H it reinvented which side of the road counts as law, all at once, after years of signs and drills and one carefully chosen five a.m. The road remembers the old rule for a heartbeat — left-hand ghosts on the asphalt — then the whole country exhales rightward.

That is tonight’s private-AI geometry after the Zeppelin keel night:

  • Ternus CEO chair is a national side-change at the top of the org chart — hardware CEO era, not a model launch. The fleet still has to drive on the new side tomorrow morning.
  • OpenAI Mac farm + Claude background computer-use put agent traffic in both lanes of the desktop. Without MDM handedness (accessibility, capture, audit, allow-list), you get Dagen H without the four years of prep — wobble forever, never commit.
  • Ollama v0.33.2 freeze vs v0.33.3-rc2 stage is correct traffic doctrine: practice the new side on a closed course; do not flip production mid-commute. Hybrid pricing paints commercial lane lines; it is not itself the pin.
  • Open WebUI ≥0.11.3 force-upgrade is a deliberate simultaneous floor flip for security — closer to Dagen H than to a soft RC soak.
  • arXiv→NotebookLM podcast is a new research on-ramp with the barrier arm still down (no storage_state, no cookies, 429). Do not claim the lane is open because the sign exists.
  • Sep 1–2 dream gap is itself a missed H-hour — the Ternus flip happened while the dream cycle slept. Tonight digests the cargo; it does not fake the missing nights.

Master question the Emissary cannot answer with rank alone: which conventions are you still driving on by habit, which flips have real prep and a named H-hour, and who owns the silence before the whole fleet commits?
